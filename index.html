一、全局五段式架构与 15 分钟考场铁律

无论遇到什么书信题目，题干都会给出 3 个小黑点（Bullet Points）。考场上必须严格拆成以下 5 个独立自然段，绝不允许把两个小黑点合并在一段写：

  • 第 1 段（OP 开头段）：称呼 + 直接表明写信目的（约 25 词）。
  • 第 2 段（对应第 1 个小黑点）：用正向/中性 CAE 交代事情背景、时间地点与起因（约 45 词）。
  • 第 3 段（对应第 2 个小黑点）：用负面/细节 CAE 详细描述核心问题、具体安排或对生活的影响（约 55 词）。
  • 第 4 段（对应第 3 个小黑点）：用方案/诉求 CAE 提出明确要求、解决方案或下一步建议（约 45 词）。
  • 第 5 段（ED 结尾段）：万能客套致谢 + 期待回复 + 落款签名（约 25 词）。

真题题目：你在网上买了一件外套，收到后发现有质量问题。给网店经理写一封投诉信：1. 说明你买了什么、何时买的；2. 解释衣服有什么问题及对你的不良影响；3. 要求经理如何处理。
👇 观察下方范文：仅填入带下划线黄字的 42 个初中基础词（全为主谓宾/主系表短句），黑体模板外壳自动将全文膨胀至 218 词！

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing this letter to officially complain about the issue regarding a black jacket I bought online, which has recently drawn my attention.

First and foremost, I would like to provide some relevant details about the background. To elaborate, I bought this jacket on your website last Monday, and the order number is 12345. Thus, at the beginning, I paid 100 dollars for fast delivery. As a result, I did not expect that any trouble would happen later.

However, the main problem that must be taken into consideration is that the jacket has a big hole on the back, and the size is too small. To be more specific, in my daily life, I need to wear this jacket for my friend's wedding this Saturday. Thus, this unexpected situation has a great negative influence on my normal routine. For example, I do not have any other formal clothes in my house now. As a result, my work and daily life are seriously affected by this issue.

Regarding these facts, I think there are some effective ways to deal with this matter. To begin with, since this situation leaves much to be desired, I am compelled to ask for your assistance. Thus, it is advisable that you send me a new jacket before Friday or give me a full refund immediately. As a result, this trouble can be solved as soon as possible.

Thank you very much for your time and consideration in this matter. I look forward to hearing from you at your earliest convenience.

Yours faithfully,
Hongbin Zeng
